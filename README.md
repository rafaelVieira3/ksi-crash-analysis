Severe Crash & High-Injury Corridor Analysis
ArcGIS Pro | SWITRS, GTFS, LA County centerlines | Summer 2026

Where do the most serious crashes happen, and how do they relate to places people walk to? I analyzed 99 killed-and-severe-injury (KSI) crashes in Redondo Beach and Torrance (SWITRS, 2021 to 2025) and compiled planning-oriented safety recommendations, presented to faculty.

Method: kernel density estimation, spatial joins, summary statistics, quarter-mile buffers, and proximity analysis. Schools and parks were digitized by hand.

Key insight: the densest crash cluster (coastal Redondo Beach, driven by pedestrian exposure) is not the highest-count corridor (Artesia Blvd, a wide arterial where speed matters), so each calls for a different safety response. Limits: counts are not normalized by traffic, and bus stops cover so much of the network that transit proximity is a weak test.

Los Angeles Urban Heat & Vegetation Analysis
Google Earth Engine | Landsat 8 Level-2 | Summer 2023 imagery

How does vegetation relate to land surface temperature across Los Angeles? I built a cloud-masked summer 2023 Landsat 8 composite for the LA area, mapped NDVI and land surface temperature, and averaged temperature within NDVI bins after removing water and very low NDVI pixels.

Average land surface temperature by NDVI bin
Average land surface temperature by NDVI bin
Takeaway: From NDVI 0.2 to 0.7, average land surface temperature fell from about 42 C to about 32 C, roughly 9 to 10 C cooler in the most vegetated areas.
